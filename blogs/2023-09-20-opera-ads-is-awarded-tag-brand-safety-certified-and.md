We’re pleased to announce that our advertising platform Opera Ads has been awarded the TAG Brand Safety Certified and Certified Against Fraud Seals by TAG (The Trustworthy Accountability Group). TAG is the leading global initiative fighting to stop criminal activity and increase trust and transparency in digital advertising.
